## 4. What is not allowed in suggestions

Suggestions must not contain:

* hate speech, slurs, or attacks on people because of who they are,
* sexual content, and especially nothing sexual involving minors,
* threats of violence or instructions for harming people or animals,
* illegal content or instructions for committing crimes,
* personal data of other people (no names, addresses, phone numbers, no
  identifying data about colleagues or ex-partners),
* spam, advertising, scams or phishing attempts,
* content that infringes copyright, trademark or privacy rights,
* anything that is not, in the operator's judgement, a suggestion about the game.

## 5. Moderation, reporting and blocking

Suggestions are reviewed by the operator before they are implemented. If a
suggestion breaks the rules above, it can be rejected, edited, or removed, and
repeated abuse can lead to a block.

* Report objectionable content: send the text of the suggestion to
  [[MODERATION_EMAIL]]. Because suggestions are not tied to an account, include
  a distinctive phrase from the suggestion so it can be found.
* Blocking: the app has no user-to-user interaction, so there is no in-app
  blocking function. The service is a single-operator moderation queue, not a
  social network. See `legal/moderation.md` for how reports are processed.
