## 2. The short version

Singular 80 is an offline game. It has **no account, no sign-in, no analytics,
no advertising and no third-party SDKs.** It collects no personal data unless
you actively send a suggestion to the service.

## 3. What the app stores on your device

On your device only, in the app's own private storage:

* your game progress, high scores and settings
* the text of suggestions you have not been able to send yet (they are queued
  locally and sent once the service is reachable again)
* the service address you entered, if you entered one

None of this leaves the device unless you send a suggestion yourself. Uninstalling
the app deletes all of it.

## 4. What is sent to the service

Only if you use the suggestion feature, and only what you type:

* the **text of the suggestion**
* the **author name** you type into the dialog — it is not linked to an account
  and not a verified identity
* the **screen the suggestion came from** (for example "Tetris")
* technical connection data that any web server receives (IP address, request
  time, user agent)

Suggestions are used to decide what gets built next. They are visible to the
operator of the service in the internal dashboard. They are **not** published in
the app without being turned into a feature first, and no suggestion is shared
with third parties.

## 5. Legal bases (GDPR)

* **Art. 6(1)(b)** — processing that is necessary to provide the suggestion
  feature you asked for.
* **Art. 6(1)(f)** — our legitimate interest in operating and securing the
  service, using the technical connection data.

There is no profiling and no automated decision-making.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Suggestions are kept in the internal dashboard as long as they are open. Once a
suggestion has been implemented, its text is kept for reference; you can ask for
it to be deleted at any time (see below). Connection data from the web server is
kept in server logs for a short period and then deleted automatically.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the
processing of your data, to data portability, and to complain to a supervisory
authority. To exercise any of these, write to [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]] with enough
detail to identify your suggestion. Requests are answered within 30 days.

Because suggestions are not tied to an account, include a distinctive phrase
from your suggestion text so we can find it.

## 8. Children

The app is rated for ages 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13,
and we knowingly collect no data from children. If you believe a child has sent
a suggestion, contact us and we will delete it.
