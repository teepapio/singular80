## Full description

Singular 80 is an Android game collection of 18 games that works completely
offline. Tetris, Texas Hold'em, FreeCell, checkers, 2048, an arena survival
game, a 3D dragon RPG with breeding, a 3D merge puzzle, a 3D bubble shooter,
a settlers-style city builder, a horse course, a crystal jumper and a
flyable dragon.

Every game is built for touch: thumbstick, on-screen buttons, keyboard and
gamepad. The 3D lobby is walkable — run to a category plaza, step onto a
pedestal, press play.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. No sign-up. The whole game collection
fits on your phone and needs no network connection.

Singular 80 is also built by the people who play it. Open any game, send a
suggestion, and your idea goes straight into the game's own backlog, tagged
with the screen it came from. Suggestions are reviewed, and the ones that make
it into a release show up in the app.

What's in it:
• Tetris with T-spins, combos and hold
• Texas Hold'em and FreeCell with hints
• Checkers, 2048 and a match-3 puzzle
• Arena survival with waves and boss runs
• 3D: dragon RPG, dragon flight, city builder, horse course, merge, bubble shooter, crystal jumper
• A walkable 3D lobby and a model gallery

Languages: English and German.
